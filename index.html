01 // Bonjour

We’re pleased
to meet you

Small and medium-sized business owners and not-for-profit administrators notoriously grasp at strings to stay on budget. We make enterprise-grade solutions accessible to them, which in turn strengthens our community and economy.

We get it

Entrepreneurs are tired of cutting corners while trying to remain competitive. Economics are a challenge for the little guy. Resource shortages and time constraints often result in half-baked software, makeshift do-it-yourself kits and lacklustre weekend crash courses.

Why we exist

Normand Consulting Group was founded in response to this. Business technology has become incredibly inexpensive, so enterprise-grade solutions are now within reach of smaller firms. What was missing was a qualified, experienced and trustworthy team to do the burdensome tasks.

How we deliver

We customize, implement and close with tailored training. Our experience comes from working for, owning or operating organizations of all sizes, including non-profits and government agencies. No smoke, no cross-selling: results on time and on budget.
