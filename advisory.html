Services // Advisory

Expertise through a lens of technology.

Research and analysis using deep, functional expertise, delivering recommended alternatives and options through a holistic perspective.

01 // Approach

Value beyond
the issue at hand

Our advisory services address both classic and innovative challenges and opportunities for entrepreneurs, such as governance, marketing, public relations, accounting and finance. Above all, our solutions are completed through a lens of technology, analytics and innovative thinking.

We provide practical knowledge and functional expertise with a comprehensive company perspective, the key to our competitive edge, delivering value across departments, leadership groups and silos.

02 // What we do

Advisory
capabilities

Nine disciplines, one framework: technology, process and probabilistic research as the control behind every recommendation.

01

Legal and Governance

Policy development and technology combined in a proprietary framework that delivers protection and guidance beyond what most large firms provide, built for customization.

  • Incorporation guidance
  • Company structure and resolutions
  • Governance documentation
  • Sustainable practices
02

Corporate and Business Strategy

We help entrepreneurs identify business goals and set short- and long-term action plans for every facet of the business. Whether status quo, growth, expansion, acquisition or divestiture is in question, technology, process and probabilistic research form the control framework.

03

Marketing Management and Customer Strategy

Research, product development, distribution, packaging, CRM and advertising. Recommendations keep corporate strategy and stakeholder needs in mind, validated with complex matrices and probabilistic methods so they are based on fact.

  • Market research and planning
  • Product development planning
  • Export development
  • Social media and communications
04

Supply Chain Design and Optimization

We deconstruct the value chain, from sourcing to market, and recommend improvements tied to specific strategic objectives, performance benchmarks and enabling technology.

05

Project Management

Skilled project leaders for your internal projects: budget control, timeline coordination and stakeholder management, with strong communication as our differentiator.

06

Business Analysis

Experienced analysts map your processes, write requirements documents and produce the business cases that lenders and partners expect before funding technology investments.

07

Finance and Accounting

Accounting process structure, including chart of accounts, processes and record templates, plus capital structure, dividend policy and risk-hedging strategy.

08

Human Resource Management

Policies and procedures for recruitment, training and compensation, usually delivered with a central database and templates.

  • Role descriptions
  • Recruitment and selection tools
  • Compensation packages
  • Employment contracts
  • Code of conduct
  • Employee handbooks
  • Training programs
  • Warning and termination letters
09

Organizational Change Management

Tailored planning to unfreeze, change and refreeze corporate practices, from planning through roll-out, with clear communication to every stakeholder group.
