Advisory Services

Expertise, through a lens of technology.

Research and analysis using deep, functional expertise, delivering recommended alternatives and options through a holistic perspective.

Our advisory services address both classic and innovative challenges and opportunities for entrepreneurs, such as governance, marketing, public relations, accounting and finance. Above all, our solutions are completed through a lens of technology, analytics and innovative thinking.

Normand Consulting Group provides practical knowledge and functional expertise with a comprehensive company perspective, which is the key to our competitive edge. We provide value beyond the issue at hand, across departments, leadership groups and silos.

What we do

Services.

01

Legal and Governance

Our legal and governance consulting solutions combine policy development and technology into a proprietary framework that delivers protection and guidance for your company. The foundational framework is built for customization: incorporation, sound company structure, resolutions and governance documentation, sustainable practices and more.

02

Corporate and Business Strategy

Small, medium and not-for-profit organizations must be strategic and calculated in achieving their goals on a timeline. We support entrepreneurs in identifying business goals and establishing short- and long-term action plans for every facet of the business.

Whether status quo, growth, expansion, acquisition or divestiture is in question, our services assign technology, process and probabilistic research as the control framework behind every strategy.

03

Marketing Management and Customer Strategy

Marketing management is the broadest domain of business: research and development, new product development, distribution, packaging, customer relationship management, advertising and more.

Our solutions keep corporate strategy and stakeholder needs in mind, using complex matrices and probabilistic validation methods so recommendations are based on fact. With the heavy lifting on our side, entrepreneurs get simple, meaningful solutions that deliver results.

04

Supply Chain Design and Optimization

Sourcing, manufacturing, packaging and distribution demand clearly and logically optimized processes. We deconstruct the entire value chain and recommend improvements tied to specific strategic objectives, performance benchmarks and enabling technology.

05

Project Management

Contract skilled project management leaders to manage your internal projects. Budget control, timeline coordination and stakeholder management, coupled with strong communication, are our differentiating PM offering.

06

Business Analysis

Qualified, experienced business analysts map your internal processes, design requirements documents and produce business cases, often required by financial institutions and partners sponsoring larger technology investments.

07

Finance and Accounting

We help structure accounting processes, including the chart of accounts, processes and record templates. Our financial advisory solutions cover capital structure policy, dividend policy and risk-hedging strategy development.

08

Human Resource Management

Policy and procedures governing recruitment and selection, training and development, and compensation programs, frequently producing a central database and document templates:

  • Role descriptions
  • Candidate application process
  • Recruitment and selection tools
  • Tailored compensation packages
  • Employment contracts
  • Code of business conduct and practices
  • Employee handbooks
  • Training materials and programs
  • Letters of warning and termination
  • Employee record and archive database
09

Organizational Change Management

Tailored planning to unfreeze, change and refreeze corporate practices. We guide planning through roll-out and advise on clear communication of strategic direction to each stakeholder group.
