Company // History and philosophy

Delivering results since 2005.

Our goal is to provide both a superior customer experience and impressive value for our customers.

01 // Our story

Entrepreneurs
deserve a break

Faced with ever increasing competition from large enterprises and an increasingly complex and dynamic global business environment, it became all too evident that the daring and hardworking leaders in small, medium and not-for-profit enterprises were struggling to adopt process and technology, despite its increasing affordability.

We believe that entrepreneurs deserve a break, and that with some guidance, simple systems implementation and integration, followed by minimal training, all Canadian businesses have an opportunity to gain substantial improvement in efficiency, cost savings and profitability.

As people are naturally resistant to change, the key to inspiring entrepreneurs to adopt this is clearly outstanding affordability.

02 // Our method

Diagnose, implement,
train

Customers first. Always.

Normand Consulting Group employs a battery of proprietary tools and processes to diagnose business needs. These tools direct our solutions to advise, train and execute simple systems and process implementations: websites, servers, e-mail systems, central company document sharing, accounting systems, marketing platforms and social media tools, to name a few.

Our customers come first and we always welcome feedback and suggestions. Use our contact page to tell us what we’re doing right or what we can improve on.
